How it works

Two ways to use it. In both, the output is a draft and a named person approves.

You have only the PO

Upload the buyer's PO. We draft the OC, CI, PL and SI from it, list the values no document gave us ("What we still need from you"), flag items with no HS code, screen the party names, and give you a record of what was checked.

You already have an OC, CI or PL

Upload them with the PO. We cross-check them against the PO and each other, field by field, and list every difference with a severity. If you upload an OC, no OC is drafted; the CI, PL and SI drafts are still produced; use or ignore them.

The engine does not assign HS codes, does not file with any authority, and does not send e-mail. Screening is names only, against one US list. See Security & data.

What the draft contains

The draft is an .xlsx in our own layout with a DRAFT watermark. Red cells are left empty for a person to fill. Your own invoice template, numbering and bank block are not supported today: you copy the checked values into your own form. The watermark is removed by you after approval.

Drafts are produced only when no HIGH difference remains. Otherwise you get BLOCKED files whose first row states the reason, so you can see what went wrong and upload a corrected set.

Security & data

Where your documents are, and where they go.

Storage

Pilot data is stored on a computer under the Provider's sole control at the Provider's premises in South Korea (not a data centre), reachable only through a Cloudflare tunnel. There is no login gate in front of the portal beyond the portal's own login. The portal address is temporary and may change; your operator sends you the current address. Encryption at rest is confirmed to you in writing before your first upload. A cloud region option is a decision pending; any move needs 14 days' e-mail notice to your signatory. Each customer's files sit in a separate workspace; another customer's shipment is not reachable from yours.

No AI model sees your documents

No document, or part of one, is sent to any AI or language model. The engine is rule-based. If we ever add AI-assisted reading, we tell you in writing before it applies to your workspace, name the provider and its retention terms, and you can decline.

What leaves the server

At most the party names, for screening, and only when the US ITA Consolidated Screening List API is enabled. In the default OFAC SDN mode the list is downloaded to the server and matched there.

Sub-processors

Cloudflare (network tunnel), the US OFAC / ITA list source (party names only, CSL mode), Google (Gmail support mailbox; Google Forms for the request form; your browser also loads fonts from Google Fonts). Any addition is notified by e-mail to your signatory 14 days before use.

Deletion

For 30 days after the pilot ends (the Return Window) you can ask for every file back, including your audit log. The workspace is deleted at the end of that window, or earlier on request. Backup copies purged within 60 days of deletion. The audit log is kept afterwards only if you instruct it, in pseudonymised form with document contents removed, and you can withdraw that instruction. Written confirmation of what was deleted and when.

Access controls in place

Per-customer folder isolation, bcrypt password hashing, signed 12-hour sessions, five failed logins lock for 15 minutes, CSRF protection, one-time invite links, upload type checks, append-only audit log, admin views banner-marked and logged.

Screening scope. Party names (buyer, consignee, notify party, bank) are checked against the US OFAC SDN list (or the US ITA Consolidated Screening List when enabled) by fuzzy name match. No EU, UK, UN or other lists. No address, country, vessel or ownership (50%) checks. A "no hits" result is not a sanctions clearance; destination embargo, licence need and end-use are not checked. The shipment page shows which list was used.

We do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, two-factor login, encryption at rest, an uptime figure or a penetration test. We are not a customs broker, forwarder, declarant or law firm.

Pilot and pricing

The pilot is free for one month, three companies at a time, by invitation. No card, no fees. After the pilot, billing is per order (one shipment reference): about US$5 to US$8 per order, no monthly fee, no minimum. Re-uploads of the same order and failed runs are free. The exact figure is set with pilot customers before anyone is charged.

What we ask in return: once the pilot agreement is signed, one redacted buyer PO uploaded in the portal before you start, so we can tell you what we read; and a 20-minute call after your first three shipments.

The pilot is operated by one person. Our target for a human reply is one business day; a target, not a guarantee. If the pilot stops, you have 30 days to take every file back, and you get written confirmation once deletion, backups included, is complete.

What this does not do (yet)

  • No customs or export declaration filing.
  • No bill of lading or certificate of origin draft; the shipping instruction lists them as documents to obtain. No dangerous-goods declaration or SDS.
  • No HS classification; items without a code are flagged only.
  • No EU, UK, UN or other non-US list, no 50% rule, no embargo, licence or end-use check.
  • No outgoing e-mail; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • No PDF reading in this pilot; no custom templates.
